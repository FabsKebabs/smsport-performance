FAQ

Good to know.

Can't see your question? Call or message us and we'll help.

Do you only work on modified cars?

No. Everyday cars are welcome. We service, diagnose and repair whatever you drive, not just project cars.

Can you collect my car?

Yes. We have our own recovery truck for breakdowns, non-runners and project cars. Call us, or send your location on WhatsApp.

How do I get a price?

Send us the job card below, or call or message with your reg and what you need. We'll give you a price before any work starts.

Will tuning affect my insurance?

Any modification, including a remap, should be declared to your insurer. We'll tell you exactly what we've changed so you can let them know.

Can you get my car ready for a track day?

Yes. From a first track day to a full drift setup, we can prepare the car and check it over before you head out.
